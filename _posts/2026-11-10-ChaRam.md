**CHẢ RAM** is a traditional Vietnamese dish made from thin rice paper, meat, and other ingredients. The rolls are deep-fried until golden and crispy.

In central Vietnam, especially Bình Định, chả ram is a popular dish. It is often served at family meals, parties, and special occasions.

The outside is crispy, while the filling is soft and savoury. Although the ingredients are simple, chả ram is full of flavour.

<!--more-->

### 1. What is CHẢ RAM?

Chả ram is a type of Vietnamese fried spring roll. It is usually smaller than many other Vietnamese spring rolls.

The main ingredients often include:

* Minced pork
* Shrimp
* Shallots
* Black pepper
* Salt and other seasonings
* Thin rice paper

Some recipes also include wood ear mushrooms or other ingredients.

The filling is wrapped in rice paper and then deep-fried. The rice paper becomes crispy and golden.

Chả ram is often served with fresh lettuce, herbs, and Vietnamese dipping sauce.

There are different versions of fried spring rolls in Vietnam. Chả ram is especially associated with Bình Định and other parts of central Vietnam.

### 2. How to Make CHẢ RAM

Making chả ram is not very difficult. However, wrapping the rolls tightly and frying them at the right temperature are important.

#### Step 1: Prepare the ingredients

Prepare minced pork, shrimp, shallots, salt, pepper, and other seasonings.

Peel and chop the shrimp into small pieces. Mix them with the pork and seasonings.

Stir everything together until the filling is well combined.

#### Step 2: Prepare the rice paper

Use thin rice paper that is suitable for frying.

Depending on the type of rice paper, you may need to soften it slightly with water. Do not make it too wet because it can tear easily.

#### Step 3: Wrap the rolls

Place a small amount of filling near one edge of the rice paper.

Fold the sides inward and roll it tightly.

Try to make the rolls similar in size. This helps them cook evenly.

#### Step 4: Deep-fry the rolls

Heat oil in a pan.

Carefully place the rolls into the hot oil. Fry them until they are golden brown and crispy.

Avoid putting too many rolls into the pan at once. Otherwise, the oil temperature may drop.

Remove the rolls and place them on a rack or paper towels to drain the excess oil.

Make sure the pork and shrimp filling is fully cooked before serving.

#### Step 5: Serve the chả ram

Serve the rolls while they are still hot.

You can eat them with fresh lettuce, herbs, and dipping sauce.

The contrast between the crispy rolls and fresh vegetables makes the dish even more delicious.

### 3. Sauce for CHẢ RAM

Vietnamese dipping sauce is an important part of this dish.

A common choice is **nước chấm**, a fish sauce-based dipping sauce.

To make a simple version, mix:

* Fish sauce
* Water
* Sugar
* Lime juice
* Minced garlic
* Chilli, if you like spicy food

Adjust the ingredients to suit your taste.

The sauce should have a balance of salty, sweet, and sour flavours.

You can dip the crispy rolls directly into the sauce. Another option is to wrap a roll in lettuce with fresh herbs and then dip it into the sauce.

Both ways are delicious!

### Final Thoughts

Chả ram is a simple but tasty Vietnamese dish.

The ingredients are easy to find, and the cooking process is straightforward. The most important things are to wrap the rolls tightly and fry them until they are crispy and fully cooked.

For me, the best part of chả ram is its texture. The crispy rice paper, savoury filling, fresh vegetables, and dipping sauce work well together.

Chả ram is also a good dish to share with family and friends. It can be served as a snack, a starter, or part of a larger Vietnamese meal.

If you visit central Vietnam, especially Bình Định, give chả ram a try. It is a delicious way to experience another part of Vietnamese food culture.
